! --- Home feed: the feed root (/feed/, with or without a query) ---
! Redirects send it to Notifications; this is the fallback without redirects. Single posts
! (/feed/update/…) are another path. Links that highlight a post keep the feed so the post shows.
www.linkedin.com##:matches-path(/^\/feed\/?(?:\?(?!(?:.*&)?highlightedUpdateUrns=urn(?::|%3[Aa])li(?::|%3[Aa])[A-Za-z]+(?::|%3[Aa])[0-9]+(?:&|,|%2[Cc]|$)).*)?$/) :is(main, aside)

! --- Feed: no further pages load ---
! Lowercase on purpose: patterns match case-insensitively, and uBO reports them lowercased.
||www.linkedin.com/flagship-web/rsc-action/actions/pagination?*sduiid=com.linkedin.sdui.pagers.feed.mainfeed$xhr,1p

! --- Home nav item: it only leads to the feed ---
! New top bar (Home is a button), then the older one still used on some pages.
www.linkedin.com##[data-testid="primary-nav"] li:has(button[aria-label^="Home"])
www.linkedin.com##li.global-nav__primary-item:has(a[href*="/feed/"])

! --- Side rails: LinkedIn News and "Add to your feed" ---
! Older layout hooks.
www.linkedin.com###feed-news-module
www.linkedin.com##[data-view-name="feed-news-module"]
www.linkedin.com##.feed-follows-module
! New layout: no stable hook. From the module's heading (a <p> there), up to the closest
! module of the rail (never past the rail itself).
www.linkedin.com##aside p:has-text(/^\s*(LinkedIn News|Add to your feed)\s*$/):upward(aside [componentkey])

! --- Side rails: "People you may know" next to profiles ---
www.linkedin.com##aside :is(h2, h3):has-text(/^\s*(People you may know|More profiles for you)/):upward(aside section)

! --- My Network: "People you may know" and other suggestion cards ---
! The page stays (invitations and your network are yours); its suggestion cards are found by
! their "Invite … to connect" and "Follow …" buttons.
www.linkedin.com##:matches-path(/^\/mynetwork\/(grow\/?)?(\?.*)?$/) button[aria-label^="Invite "][aria-label$=" to connect"]:upward(:is(li, [role="listitem"]))
www.linkedin.com##:matches-path(/^\/mynetwork\/(grow\/?)?(\?.*)?$/) button[aria-label^="Follow "]:upward(:is(li, [role="listitem"]))

! --- Promoted: ads in the side rails ---
www.linkedin.com##aside div:has(> iframe[title="advertisement"])
www.linkedin.com##section:has(> iframe[data-ad-banner])
